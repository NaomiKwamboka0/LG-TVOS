Family TV for Windows: what is inside and under which licence

FamilyTV.exe      Family TV itself. Uses Monocypher (licence in
                  Monocypher-LICENCE.md) and the Inter typeface (SIL Open
                  Font Licence, Inter-LICENSE.txt).
SDL2.dll          Simple DirectMedia Layer 2.32.10, zlib licence
                  (SDL2-LICENSE.txt). https://www.libsdl.org
libmpv-2.dll      mpv video player library, built by shinchiro
                  (https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake).
                  GNU General Public Licence, version 2 or later; it
                  includes FFmpeg and other libraries under GPL/LGPL.
                  Source: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv and the build
                  scripts at the address above. Licence text:
                  https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.txt
yt-dlp.exe        yt-dlp 2026.10.26, public domain (The Unlicense).
                  https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
deno.exe          Deno 2.9.8, MIT licence. https://github.com/denoland/deno

This copy is for the family's own use. Because libmpv is GPL, anyone who
passes the program on to others must also offer them the source code of
