Kenyan TV channels, world news and live sport from the stations' own YouTube streams, plus games for the kids and parent controls. Free and open source (GPL-3.0).

**Watch the 30-second tour:** `familytv-promo.mp4` below.

## Download

| Your computer | File | |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | `FamilyTV-Setup-<version>.exe` | Installer with Start menu and desktop shortcuts |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 15+ | `FamilyTV-<version>-macos.dmg` | Open it, drag Family TV to Applications |

**First start.** Family TV is not signed by a company yet, so the computer asks once:
- **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC" → **More info** → **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** "cannot check it for malicious software" → **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security** → **Open Anyway**.

Live channels need internet; a channel takes 10–15 seconds to start. Games work offline.

**Keys:** arrows to move, Enter = OK, Backspace or Esc = Back, F = full screen, Q = quit.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.
