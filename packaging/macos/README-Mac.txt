FAMILY TV FOR MAC
=================

Needs:   a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 15 Sequoia or
         newer.

Install: open the .dmg and drag Family TV onto the Applications folder.

Start:   the first time, macOS says it cannot check Family TV for
         malicious software (Family TV is not signed by a company, so
         Apple does not know it yet). Click "Done", then open System
         Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
         "Open Anyway" next to Family TV. After that it opens normally.

Keys:    Arrows      move around / channel up and down / volume
         Return      OK (choose; while watching: the channel list)
         Delete/Esc  Back (while watching: back to the home screen)
         F           full screen on and off
         Q           close Family TV

Live channels play from each station's YouTube live stream, so they need
internet. A channel can take 10-15 seconds to start. Going back to the
home screen stops the stream, so no data is used while you are there.

Data use per hour of watching (Parent settings, "Live TV picture"):
         360p  about 0.5 GB      480p  about 0.6 GB (default)
         720p  about 1.4 GB

The family's settings, PIN and saves live in
~/Library/Application Support/FamilyTV/FamilyTV and are kept when the app is
replaced by a newer one.
